## Principle

Each `roles.*` channel owns a **persistent agent** (character limb, companion, fauna, or scenic spirit) for the life of a section or song. Intensity of the role = how strongly that agent acts — not a random particle layer with no identity. Lyrics still pick WHAT world; roles pick WHICH agents punch/glow/fog inside it.

**Intentional pairing:** one lead body (from cast roster) is the hub; other roles attach as limbs, companions, or environment spirits — same story family, not a new mascot every hit.

| Role | Persistent agent | What it does when the role rises | Cast-roster hooks |

| `bass` | **Weight / scale / shadow** of the main body **or** a **low beast** | Vignette and world-scale breathe with the body; low beast prowls behind/under in dark/chaos | Shadow of lead; `void_presence` / low beast only when vibe is scary/chaos |

## Persistence rules

1. **Bind at section start** (or song start): assign agents once; don’t reshuffle every onset.  
2. **Lead is hub:** all other agents orbit or attach to the lyric figure’s ground plane.  
3. **Spoken mode:** lead + pads environment only; kick/snare/hats agents muted or gesture-only at floor (matches spoken clamps).  
4. **Peace:** hats→fauna; harsh agent absent; bass = soft shadow not beast.  
5. **Chaos:** drum-warden + harsh ash allowed; still one lead crosshair.  
6. **Scary:** bass may be low beast; hats sparse; harsh rare startle on dread figure only.  
7. **Chorus:** snare companion may multiply into formation accents; lead stays readable.  
8. **HOLD:** karaoke/scenic type and cast visibility stay on — agents *explain* roles; they don’t replace text or hide people.

---

## Anti-patterns
- Random sparks with no owner  
- Harsh ash on peaceful nature cast  
- New mascot every snare hit  
- Hiding lyric type to show agents  
- Fake chorus agents during spoken  
- Bass beast in pop candy peace
