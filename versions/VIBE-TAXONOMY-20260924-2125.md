## Core rule

**Congruence first.** If the ear hears peace, the eye must get nature / soft light / heal. Chaos earns fracture only at psych peaks. Scary stays sparse and dark — startle rare (Juslin brain-stem; Huron reaction). Spoken word is intimate meaning-world, never a fake chorus.

## Primary vibes

### A) Peaceful → peace
| Axis (emotion) | Typical | LOOK |
|----------------|---------|------|
| valence | + | soft, open |
| arousal | low–mid | slow camera, long morph |
| aggression / harsh | ~0 | no ash, no glitch bed |
| hope / pads | high | fog wash, soft shafts |
| kick | sparse silk | ground breath, not punch spam |

**Worlds:** `forest`, `ocean`, `snow`, `dream_clouds`, soft `empty_highway` dusk, gentle `cathedral_space` light — **nature + animals** when packs allow (birds, distant herd silhouette, fish/light motes as fauna proxies).  
**Cast:** one calm figure or none; heal posture (standing in light, hands open) — never combat.  
**Type:** integrated scenic; long holds; expire gently.  
**Camera:** drift/float; no dutch; minimal shake.  
**Forbidden:** red_void storm spam, harsh etch, formation war geometry, continuous white-out.

### B) Chaos → chaos (earned)
| Axis | Typical | LOOK |
|------|---------|------|
| arousal / flux | high | faster events |
| aggression / harsh | high | fracture, ash, glitch |
| kick / drop | present | punches + rare gate |

**Worlds:** `storm`, `industrial_tunnel`, `burning_desert`, `red_void`, warzone-leaning ruins — fracture architecture.  
**Cast:** unstable formation, running, falling; BG chaos around center (This Is America grammar) but still one crosshair.  
**Type:** may etch on harsh; don’t karaoke through debris.  
**Camera:** higher cut/morph rate at peaks only; shake on kick; whip at gates.  
**Psych lock:** chaos peaks are **earned** (build → one gate) — not chaos from bar 1 if arrangement is still verse (Salimpoor anticipation / Huron tension). Soft verse inside a chaotic song still plants motifs before the break.

### C) Scary → scary

**Cast:** sparse figures (one distant walker, empty doorway silhouette); hide more than reveal.  
**Type:** quiet integrated whispers; expire; silence between lines.  
**Camera:** slow push into dread; dutch ok for tension; startle whip **rare** then re-hold.  
**Forbidden:** euphoric hope palette, animal cute fauna, bright chorus formation unless lyric irony demands it (then brief and cold).

## Spoken word / non-music mode

### Detection cues (for Audio Pulse — heuristic, not ML)
Speech-like / non-groove when several hold:
- Low / unstable `bpm` confidence OR weak periodic kick pocket
- High `vocalish` / VocalEstimate vs low kick+bass groove
- Low groove proxy: weak entrainment (few stable beat ticks), mid-band speech energy
- Long `silence` gaps between phrases
- User/mood or lyrics density without instrumental chorus shape

**Mode name:** `spoken` / `intimateSpeech` (implementers pick field).

### LOOK when spoken / non-groove
- **Intimate:** human scale, close figure or empty nature void
- **Lyric-as-world:** EVERY scenic choice from word meaning (Lyrics Brain concepts) — rain line → rainy world, not a drop light show
- **Minimal punch/flash:** kick/snare multipliers near floor; no fake chorus open; no white-out
- **Noise/silence:** soft ambient `white_void` / `forest` / `ocean` / `dream_clouds` — **never** invent a chorus plateau
- **Type:** scenic/integrated, long holds for comprehension; expire between thoughts
- **Camera:** still / slow drift; no beat shake

### Anti-patterns
| Fake | Instead |
|------|---------|
| Speech treated as drop | Hold intimate; scenery from words |
| Silence → black strobe | Soft ambient void/nature |
| Applause/noise → harsh party | Gentle pad fog or stay |
| Karaoke lower-third only | Words in world + quiet figure |

## Congruence checklist (Director / Visual / Worlds)
1. What is the dominant vibe *now* (peace / chaos / scary / spoken / groove / melancholy / hope)?  
2. Does the preset pack match? If not, morph within family or gate-swap once.  
3. Are characters staging the vibe (calm / fracture / sparse dread / intimate speech)?  
4. Is flash earned by psych peak, or lying about the feel?
