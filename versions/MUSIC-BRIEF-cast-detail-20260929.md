## 1) Costume silhouette @ LED / phone distance
- **Read the coat, not the face.** At phone/LED scale, fans parse **outer silhouette** first: coat flare, cloak drape, jacket shoulders, skirt/cape wedge, boots/heel stance — not eyes, logos, or fine embroidery (This Is America / Formation grammar: readable body + wardrobe wedge from far).
- **Trim = one accent, not texture soup.** Belt, collar flash, cuff stripe, or cape edge as a **single** high-contrast rim against the world; skip micro-pattern and facial detail that muddy into noise on a 6″ screen.
- **Archetype owns the wedge.** `fg_performer` / `pop_candy` → sharp jacket or candy coat flare; `pastoral_walker` → soft cloak/coat long line; `sacred_solitary` → robe/column; `chaos_fracture` → torn coat / asymmetric scrap — still one readable outline, not particle glitter.
- **Continuity:** same silhouette family verse→chorus (bigger trim / brighter rim on chorus), never a full wardrobe swap mid-phrase.

## 2) Dance vs sway vs still (energy / kick / role)
- **Dance (freestyle / formation):** high kick density + groove pocket + joy/club/chorus lift (pop, K-pop, latin, afro, EDM hooks) — `danceIntent` high; limbs readable, formation on chorus peaks.
- **Sway:** mid energy, R&B/soul/reggae/jazz pocket, warm pads — body follows kick/bass gently; no laser-arms; `danceIntent` mid.
- **Still / micro-gesture:** spoken, ambient, classical adagio, scary sparse, ballad confession — kick/snare near floor; lyric figure holds; `danceIntent` low / still (VIBE spoken + DANCE anti-patterns).
- **Role bias:** kick/bass footfalls drive sway-or-dance; hats = spark accents not full choreo; harsh/chaos cast = pulse/glitch body only when vibe is chaos — never force dance on pastoral/sacred/spoken.

## Anti-patterns
- Face close-up detail as the costume read  
- Constant dance on spoken / dread / ambient  
- Outfit swap that breaks silhouette continuity
