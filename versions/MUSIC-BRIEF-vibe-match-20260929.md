## Ladder (live energy + harsh + kick → imagery)

### 0) Peaceful
- **Ear:** soft pads, sparse silk kick, low harsh, low arousal  
- **Eye:** forest / ocean / snow / dream clouds / soft highway dusk; heal light; pastoral walker or calm tableau; slow morph; no ash  
- **Motion:** drift camera; sway or still; bloom silk  

### 1) Warm / groove (not aggression)
- **Ear:** steady pocket, mid kick, low harsh  
- **Eye:** rainy city night, warm neon street, ocean dusk; runner / freestyle body — **not** warzone  
- **Motion:** entrained bounce; medium cut rate  

### 2) Tense
- **Ear:** rising flux, snare chatter, pre-chorus build, mid arousal, harsh still low–mid  
- **Eye:** industrial soft, storm edge, cold ruins, dutch lean; cast braced / walk faster; motif tighten  
- **Motion:** push-in; shorter morphs; hold white-out  

### 3) Aggressive / chaos (earned)
- **Ear:** high harsh + high kick/energy storms, metal/punk/noise/EDM peak, vibe.chaos / aggression high  
- **Eye:** storm / industrial tunnel / burning desert / red void / warzone / fracture cast; ash on harsh; ground punch on kick; chaos_fracture / fg_performer pulse — **not** meadow or candy void  
- **Motion:** shake on kick; faster events at peaks only; one drop gate then plateau (psych)  

### 4) Scary (sibling, not “more aggressive party”)
- **Ear:** cold sparse + rare spikes  
- **Eye:** dread void / staircase / isolation — threat implied; startle rare  
- **≠** aggressive festival chaos
