## Outfit / look themes (by vibe)

Themes are palette + silhouette language for Visual Engine / Worlds light — not branded cosplay.

### Peace
- **Linen dawn:** soft neutrals, loose silhouette, warm soft key  
- **Moss trail:** greens/earth, hood or cloak read as nature blend  
- **Water gloss:** blues/silvers, reflective wet-street peace variant  

### Groove / neon
- **After Hours red:** dark coat + red practical wash (Blinding Lights)  
- **Neon trim:** black base + cyan/magenta edge light  
- **Highway dust:** travel jacket, motion blur friendly silhouette  

### Pop candy
- **Chrome candy:** high-sat pastels, clean graphic silhouette  
- **Stage gloss:** sequin/specular hits on chorus only  
- **Color-block crew:** formation members share one accent color  

### Chaos
- **Fracture rag:** torn edges, asymmetric, ash dusting  
- **Industrial hazard:** hard silhouette, cold practicals  
- **Ember coat:** charcoal + ember rim (fire climb)  

### Scary
- **Dread coat:** long dark coat, face soft/obscured  
- **Pale void:** near-white minimal figure on dark (or inverse)  
- **Threshold:** figure half in doorway shadow  

### Spoken intimate
- **Room clothes:** ordinary hoodie/shirt — anti-spectacle  
- **Desk lamp key:** single warm practical, rest falloff  
- **Empty chair:** optional no-body; garment on chair as proxy  

### Sacred / cosmic
- **Aisle linen:** simple robe/long coat in shaft light  
- **Star dust:** dark body + particle rim into space outro

## Pairing rules

1. **Vibe picks archetype family first** (peace→nature_fauna/pastoral; scary→dread_sparse; spoken→spoken_intimate; etc.).  
2. **Outfit theme must match vibe** — no chrome candy on scary unless lyric irony (then 1 phrase max).  
3. **Verse:** 1 of `tableau_figure` / `pastoral_walker` / `spoken_intimate` / `dread_sparse`.  
4. **Chorus:** widen to `formation_crew` or heroic single (`neon_runner` / `pop_candy` / `fg_performer`).  
5. **Drop gate:** hold archetype; may switch outfit rim to harsh/ember for 1 beat then restore.  
6. **Animals:** `nature_fauna` for peace beds; never cute fauna on scary/chaos peaks.  
7. **Spoken:** never auto-upgrade to `formation_crew` or `pop_candy`.

## Anti-patterns
- Empty world when characters ON  
- Pop candy outfits on peaceful nature tracks  
- Formation spam in verse  
- Karaoke sticker people (no feet on ground plane)  
- Fake chorus cast upgrade during spoken mode
