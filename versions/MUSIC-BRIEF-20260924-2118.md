## WHY (one paragraph)

Music videos that feel like movies keep a **human crosshair** in a living world (This Is America FG / Formation tableaux / Blinding Lights runner) and put words **into** that world as architecture, graffiti, neon, or etched texture (Bass situational type, West Side Story wall credits, Cooper etched psyche) — not as a permanent lower-third karaoke layer. On a phone LED, empty scenery + caption bar reads as a lyric video; figure + environmental type reads as the head-movie.

---

## Scene Director

- **Always stage characters when characters are ON:** verse = single silhouette path (walker under streetlights, lone highway figure); chorus = widen to formation / multiple silhouettes OR heroic single; breakdown = distant/small figure.
- **World owns continuity; people own meaning:** same pack family verse→chorus; escalate BG weather/chaos around a stable center figure (Murai grammar).
- **Lyric concepts choose WHAT the figure is doing** (kneel, run, look up at tear in sky) — not just which preset ID.
- Hard-cut still only at gates; morph figures with world (don’t teleport the person).
- Instrument-owned gaps (pre-lyric air, drop vocal-out, hush): may hold figure silent with no new type — music owns the frame.

**Prompts:** Prefer rainy_city / empty_highway / futuristic_city / cathedral / ruins / space with **drawPreset character branches**; forbid empty postcard for narrative songs.

## Visual Engine

- Typography layouts already: `integrated` | `cinematic` | `title` — push **integrated** harder for intro/outro/verse anchors; chorus `title` must still feel **world-lit** (glow from shafts/neon), minimize permanent opaque karaoke plate when contrast allows.
- **No purple/colored karaoke chase bars** — lead/VocalEstimate glow only (matches system comments).
- Characters flag (`showCharacters`) should drive silhouette density; couple `lead` beams to figure chest/head, not random screen center empty of body.
- Drop: figure readable within ~0.5–1 beat after white-out; type may etch/`harsh` then restore.
- Hats sparks / pads fog = environment around body; kick = ground under feet.

---

## Camera

- **Crosshair the human** (Fury Road): push-in toward figure on `build`; shake on kick without losing silhouette.
- Frame for character staging: intimate verse = tighter on figure; chorus = pull back to show formation / world open.
- Dutch only when figure’s world is tense; clear dutch when chorus resolves.
- Whip at section gates may reveal new wall/sign plane for environmental type.

---

## Worlds Presets

- Every Priority A–D pack that supports narrative should have a **clear character staging plane**: wet street for walker, highway vanishing point for runner, cathedral aisle for solitary figure, ruins for ash-kneel, space for dissolve.
- Add / polish **signage / neon / wall / glass planes** as hooks for environmental typography (Bass / West Side Story steal).
- Pack family stays verse→chorus; character costume/light can change color with motif without swapping planet.
- Void packs (`white_void` / `red_void`): still stage a figure — void is costume for the body, not an excuse for text-only.
