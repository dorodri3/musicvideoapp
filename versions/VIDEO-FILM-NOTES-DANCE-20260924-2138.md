## When dance wins vs stillness wins

| Context | Dance reads as meaning | Stillness wins |
|---------|------------------------|----------------|
| Groove / euphoric chorus | Yes — formation or freestyle | — |
| Joy / ritual celebration | Yes — rhythmic group or lone joy | — |
| Club / neon night | Yes — sway, runner, pop candy | — |
| Spoken / non-groove | — | Intimate still or micro-gesture only |
| Scary / dread | — | Sparse freeze; rare startle twitch |
| Pastoral peace | Soft sway or fauna motion only | Prefer calm stand/walk |
| Pre/build tension | Contained pulse | Avoid full chorus dance early |
| Drop gate | 1-beat hit pose | Not a dance break unless lyric says |

**Rule:** Dance is a **cast action language** gated by vibe + section + groove — never auto-dance every kick on a dread/spoken track.
