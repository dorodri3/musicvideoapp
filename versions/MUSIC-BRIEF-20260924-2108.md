## How each role should feel

Proxies are heuristic (not stems). Feel = attack, sustain, space, aggression vs silk — then map to the stable visual verbs in ROLES.md.

### Verse (narrative walk — intensity below chorus)

| Role | Should feel | Why scenery / light should respond that way |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `kick` | Sparse, soft floor — pocket, not club. Long gaps between punches. | Scene is storytelling. Constant kick-shake reads as “party in a verse.” Horizon punch only on phrase downbeats so the world stays readable. |
| `bass` | Warm sustain, mid weight — body under the vocal, not sub-drop. | Slight vignette / world scale holds the frame intimate→human. Heavy bass bloom would steal lyric focus. |
| `snare` | Dry, mid, conversational — backbeat or off accents, short decay. | Mid punch flash = punctuation between lines, not white-outs. Keeps camera language calm (dolly/drift, not shake). |
| `hats` | Silk ticks / light air — texture, not glitter storms. | Sparks / rain needles at low density = atmosphere (city rain, dust) without filling negative space the verse needs. |
| `pads` | Wide, slow attack, soft sustain — the air the verse breathes in. | Sky / fog wash establishes palette and weather; this is the bed under lyric meaning. |
| `lead` / `vocalish` | Center, clear, caress — phrases land then rest. Blend with `VocalEstimate`. | Center beams + lyric emphasis should follow sung lines; verse type is large but not huge so chorus can escalate. |
| `harsh` | Near zero — grit only if the lyric/mix is already abrasive. | Glitch/ash in a soft verse breaks trust; save shock for pre/drop. |

Musical reason: verses plant motifs and carry plot. Dynamics sit under the chorus. Visual density must leave headroom or the chorus cannot open.

### Chorus (open / significant — motif bigger on each repeat)

| Role | Should feel | Why scenery / light should respond that way |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `kick` | Present, punchy, regular — ground of the anthem. | Horizon punch + controlled shake = “we’re here.” Scale can step cinematic→epic; still not strobe every sample. |
| `bass` | Fuller, deeper — world weight expands. | Vignette + scale sell the open; bass is why the world feels larger, not just brighter particles. |
| `snare` | Harder crack, maybe clap stack — celebration / affirmation. | Mid flash denser; pairs with bigger typography on hook lines. |
| `hats` | Brighter, busier — lift and forward motion. | Sparks escalate; treble lift reads as hope/arousal without needing a new preset every bar. |
| `pads` | Bigger bloom, more sustain — sky opens. | Fog/sky wash widens; chorus “open” is harmonic/register as much as drums. |
| `lead` / `vocalish` | Declare — sustained, forward, huge hook energy. | Center beams + **huge** type on chorus phrases; motif callbacks from verse return stronger (`chorus.repeat`). |
| `harsh` | Optional spice — only if the mix is aggressive or lyric turns dark. | Ash/glitch as accent, not bed; otherwise chorus stays euphoric/open per emotion engine bias. |

Musical reason: chorus is release + statement. Same world as the verse, **bigger** — not a random new planet. Director intents already say motif establish → bigger on repeat → max on final; roles must supply the energy curve that justifies that scale.

### Drop / breakdown impact (pre→drop gate, or EDM-style hit)

Treat `audio.drop` + sudden role spikes as the gate. A “drop” is a **short structural shock**, not a 12-second continuous mode (a held loud chorus is a plateau — see song briefs).

| Role | Should feel | Why scenery / light should respond that way |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `kick` | Maximum attack, short — the body hit. | One hard ground punch + camera shake at the gate; then either settle into chorus pocket or rest. Repeating max kick forever = numbness. |
| `bass` | Surge then hold or cut — sub pressure. | World-scale spike / vignette crush at impact; lighting can white-out **once** with the gate. |
| `snare` | Flam / fill into the hit, then space or half-time. | Mid flash as the fill; after the gate, leave air so the kick owns the downbeat. |
| `hats` | Often cut or open-hat whoosh into the hit — then sparse or 16ths depending on genre. | Spark burst on the gate, then either glitter bed (big-room) or silence (trap/halftime). Match the arrangement, don’t invent hats. |
| `pads` | Duck or filter-open into the hit — tension then bloom. | Sky wash can blackout→bloom with the drop; supports `build`→`drop` envelopes. |
| `lead` / `vocalish` | May silence for the hit (“vocal chop out”) or scream the title — song-dependent. | If vocals drop out, let instruments own the frame; if the hook lands on the drop, keep beams/type. Lyrics Brain decides which. |
| `harsh` | Peak allowed — noise riser, distortion, impact crack. | Glitch bars / ash / storm crack **at the gate**; decay fast so the post-drop world is readable. |

Musical reason: a drop works because of contrast. Pre builds tension (`build`, snare fills, pad filter); the hit spends it. Visuals must underplay the pre (or hold energy without climaxing), spend once, then either ride a chorus plateau or collapse into breakdown hush.

### Quick section cheat-sheet

| Section | Dominant roles | Visual posture |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Intro | pads, light hats, rare kick | Intimate reveal, negative space |
| Verse | lead/vocalish + pads + light rhythm | Narrative, plant motifs |
| Pre | rising hats/snare, bass pressure, build | Tension ramp — no full white-out yet |
| Chorus | kick+bass+lead, brighter hats/pads | Open scale, huge type, motif return |
| Drop gate | kick + harsh (+ bass surge) | One shock; then commit to chorus or hush |
| Breakdown | pads, sparse kick, maybe harsh ash | Rest, silk, lyric or pure music owns frame |
| Outro | pads/treble air, fading kick | Dissolve / callback, don’t re-climax |

## Concrete prompts per field

### Scene Director
- Drive section intents from structure (`intro/verse/pre/chorus/drop/breakdown/outro`); use `chorus.repeat` for motif escalation.
- Verse = develop world + plant 2–4 motifs; chorus = same world opened; drop = single gate event (preset shock or lighting event), not a new random preset every bar.
- Scale arc via ScaleDirector: verse ≤ human/cinematic; chorus → cinematic/epic; drop may flash epic; breakdown returns intimate/human.
- Prefer continuity morphs over hard cuts except at drop/thunder-class events.
- Read song-specific MUSIC-BRIEF-* when present; this file is the default WHY.

### Visual Engine
- Keep ROLES.md verbs stable: kick→ground/horizon+shake, bass→scale/vignette, snare→mid flash, hats→sparks/needles, pads→sky fog, lead→beams+lyric, harsh→glitch/ash.
- Envelope by section: verse low multipliers; chorus high but readable; drop = one-shot peaks (`_lastWhiteFlash` discipline — don’t spam white-out).
- Typography: verse large; chorus huge on hook lines; after drop if vocal-out, ease type and let layers speak.
- Quality/60fps: prefer fewer, stronger role reactions over many weak ones.

### Audio Pulse
- Expose clean `roles.*` + `build`/`drop`/`silence`/`beat` per ROLES.md (already live).
- Thresholding: verse — higher kick/onset thresholds so quiet storytelling doesn’t false-shake; chorus — balanced; into drop — let `build` ramp, fire `drop` as a short pulse not a sticky high.
- Blend `VocalEstimate` into lead emphasis; never replace `roles.lead`.
- When energy plateaus high (held chorus), keep role dynamics alive with micro-variation — don’t freeze at 1.0.

### Lyrics Brain
- Concepts = WHAT world; never override role physics.
- Mark lines that should own the frame (hook, image-heavy metaphors) vs gaps where instruments own (pre-lyric air, drop vocal-out, post-chorus hush, outro tail).
- On drop: if lyric lands on the hit, keep concept+type; if vocal drops out, suppress new concepts for 1–2 bars so kick/harsh own the gate.
- Prefer concept-graph presets that match the planted motifs so chorus callbacks work.
