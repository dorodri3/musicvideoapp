## Top 5 quality gaps (actionable)

### 1. Kill bass-as-building-height — **Visual Engine + Worlds Presets**
`presets.js` still scales skyline / silhouette height from `audio.bass`. Reads as EQ bars made of buildings.  
**Fix:** lock silhouette geometry; drive bass into wet-street pulse, window flicker, vignette weight, or camera push — never building height.

### 2. Retarget lead “spot wedges” — **Visual Engine + Lighting / Camera**
Renderer still draws five fixed full-frame lead beams. Stage-rig, not subject light.  
**Fix:** one scenic accent per world (road-center glow on highway, stained bloom in cathedral, moon flare on ocean) driven by `rolesIntent.leadBeam`.

### 3. Soft-clip LED / white wash so drama doesn’t flatten the world — **Visual Engine + Lighting**
Chorus/breakdown washes blow out skyline (shots 03–04). White is allowed; unchecked wash is not.  
**Fix:** cap simultaneous wash+bloom+beam+flash; prefer short white_flash then settle into a darker readable world; keep window/grid detail visible through beams (outro already proves this works).

### 4. Demote spectrum/EQ texture — **Audio Pulse + Visual Engine**
Verse shot shows clear horizontal equalizer-like bars under the scene.  
**Fix:** keep `spectrum` analysis-only (or hidden debug). Instrument energy must become scenic verbs (kick = ground settle, hats = densify *existing* rain, pads = sky grade) — never a bar graph layer in the phone show.

### 5. Make motifs distinctive + expire sticky lyrics — **Scene Director + Lyrics Brain + Visual Engine**
Motif draw still leans generic circles/ellipses/strokes; sticky `_lastGood` has no expiry (risk: stale phrase across long instrumental).  
**Fix:** map `directive.symbols` / motifId families to 1–2 persistent scenic props that scale with motifStrength; sticky hold expires on section change or after N seconds without vocal.
