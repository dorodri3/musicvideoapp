## Core rule (Salimpoor 2011 + Huron 2006)

**Spend climax once.** Anticipation (`build` / pre) earns dopamine; peak (chorus open / drop gate) spends it; repeating max flash destroys contrastive reward. Same-world motif enlargement = prediction pleasure; random new planet every bar = expectancy failure.

---

## Scene Director

- **Pre:** Hold morph; raise tension (scale/speed) — do not open the chorus world early (anticipation window).
- **Chorus:** Same motifs **bigger** (prediction reward + Formation grammar); lift without karaoke caption spam.
- **Drop:** One reaction gate → commit; then plateau or hush.
- **Breakdown:** Soothe / heal — pads weather, distant figure, expire aggressive concepts.
- **Motif returns** on `chorus.repeat` / outro = episodic memory hits (nostalgia).
- Dark verse: linger on figure+scenic type; bright chorus: gesture/formation over dense words.

## Visual Engine

- White-out / harsh etch: **at peak or gate only**; restore readable world in ~0.5–1 beat (reaction → appraisal).
- Chorus lift = saturation + scale + world-lit huge type — **not** continuous strobe (protect groove inverted-U / medium density).
- Negative-valence passages: longer phrase hold, integrated type; positive: fewer words, bigger weight.
- After destruction (storm/ash): heal with pads fog / hope palette — soothe earns next peak.
- Kick pulse entrains ground under figure; don’t false-shake in low-arousal verse (spoils pocket).

## Audio Pulse

- Raise kick/onset hysteresis in low-arousal / verse (protect storytelling attention).
- `build` must ramp cleanly into pre→chorus (caudate anticipation window).
- `drop` = short impulse paired with harsh at gate — not sticky high (one reaction, not seizure).
- Silence floors = soothe / negative space for appraisal and lyric expire.
- VocalEstimate blends into lead for lyric-attention windows; never replace `roles.lead`.

## Camera

- Push-in tracks tension/build; **ease** when chorus prediction resolves (open world).
- Shake = kick entrainment amplitude / drop burst only — groove pulse, not startle spam.
- Dutch = tension only; clear on release.
- Hold longer on figure in sad/dark lyric windows (analytic attention); pull back for bright chorus formation.
- After startle gate: re-crosshair human immediately (appraisal / readability).

## Lyrics Brain (psych attention)

- Dark/sad: keep scenic blocks longer; fewer concept swaps.
- Happy/chorus: one hook phrase huge; expire text so imagery+characters breathe.
- Instrument-owned gaps after peaks: suppress new lyrics 1–2 bars (let NAc peak finish visually).
