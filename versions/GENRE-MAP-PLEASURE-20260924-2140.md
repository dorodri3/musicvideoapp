# GENRE MAP — Fan pleasure / head-movie chase
**When:** 2026-09-24 21:40 JST

**Author:** Music Researcher  
**CEO deepen:** not labels — what fans LOVE and chase when they close their eyes  
**Folds:** GENRE-MAP-20260924-2139 · VIBE · CAST · ROLE-AGENTS · DANCE · WAR · PSYCH  
**Weapons:** still gated — pleasure does not unlock guns  

For each family: **core pleasure** · **body feeling** · **lyric role** · **world / cast / dance** · **anti-patterns** (what would feel wrong).

---

## 1) Pop
- **Core pleasure:** Hook clarity; chorus as the earworm sky opening; sparkle you can hum tomorrow.
- **Body:** Light bounce → arms-up lift on chorus; smile-muscle more than fist.
- **Lyric role:** Melodic slogan owns the frame on chorus; verses plant identity/place; type becomes scenery for the hook.
- **World / cast / dance:** Soft neon city, candy void, dream clouds; `pop_candy` / `formation_crew`; freestyle→formation on chorus.
- **Anti-patterns:** Metal ash as default; fake dread; murky illegible hook type; weapons; spoken-mode clamp on a dance-pop track.

## 2) Hip-hop / rap
- **Core pleasure:** Flow & bar energy; 808 weight in the chest; cipher/block presence; wordplay that blooms into imagery.
- **Body:** Head-nod pocket; chest thud from kick/808; swagger walk more than EDM arms-up (unless trap anthem chorus).
- **Lyric role:** Bars ARE the world — punchlines, flex, story, confession; scenic type rides the rhyme, never karaoke empty.
- **World / cast / dance:** Rainy city, tunnel, night highway, block tableau; `fg_performer`, cipher-adjacent companions; freestyle body; formation on hooks if anthem.
- **Anti-patterns:** Soft ambient wash that kills the pocket; candy K-pop formation on a dark street narrative; inventing guns for “street” without lyric gate; fake four-on-floor if the beat is boom-bap swing.

## 3) R&B / soul
- **Core pleasure:** Intimacy; velvet closeness; ache that soothes; voice as skin-distance.
- **Body:** Slow sway, breath, chest warmth — not club bounce unless neo-soul groove says so.
- **Lyric role:** Confession / desire / healing; long holds; lyric figure close; pads as aura around the singer.
- **World / cast / dance:** Night rain room, soft cathedral, ocean dusk, dream clouds; `tableau_figure`, intimate freestyle; stillness in ballads.
- **Anti-patterns:** Strobe chaos; metal harsh; formation cheer squad; weapons; cold white-void spectacle.

## 4) Rock / metal / punk
- **Core pleasure:** Power — amp in the ribs; punk spit-joy or metal earned catharsis; riff as character.
- **Body:** Headbang / jump / fist; ground punch from kick; harsh as texture of rage or ecstasy.
- **Lyric role:** Anthem shout or myth story; chorus = banner; verses can snarl.
- **World / cast / dance:** Industrial, storm, desert burn, ruins, red void; `chaos_fracture`, `fg_performer`; freestyle pulse; formation rare.
- **Anti-patterns:** Soft gospel glow as default; pastoral animals on blast-beat; candy sparkle that undercuts power; unearned constant white-out.

## 5) Country / folk / americana
- **Core pleasure:** Story & place; road / porch / small-town intimacy; twang warmth; honest landscape.
- **Body:** Walk, sway on the porch, truck-cab nod — not laser-club.
- **Lyric role:** Narrative owns the movie — names, towns, trucks, heartbreak; place words should steer world pack.
- **World / cast / dance:** Empty highway, desert, forest, snow, soft ruins; `pastoral_walker`, `tableau_figure`; walk ≫ club dance.
- **Anti-patterns:** EDM drop grammar; futuristic candy void; formation spectacle; weapons drip; speech clamp killing a sung story ballad (keep story readable, not silent).

## 6) Jazz / blues
- **Core pleasure:** Space — the breath between notes; cool ache; improvisation as living negative space.
- **Body:** Sway, snap, lean — pocket without four-on-floor hammer.
- **Lyric role:** Often secondary to instrumental conversation; when sung, smoky confession; don’t force pop hook typography.
- **World / cast / dance:** Rainy city, soft cathedral, smoky tunnel, dream clouds; `tableau_figure`; sway not bounce.
- **Anti-patterns:** Drop gates; laser walls; K-pop formation; harsh war ash; busy karaoke.

## 7) Classical / orchestral / score
- **Core pleasure:** Architecture & swell — themes return; scale grows with arrangement; hush that earns thunder.
- **Body:** Stillness → spine lift on crescendo; ritual breath, not dance-floor.
- **Lyric role:** Rare / choral / programmatic; when present, title-like integrated type; motif > slogan.
- **World / cast / dance:** Cathedral, space, ocean, snow, staircase, soft void; `sacred_solitary`, `cosmic_dissolve`; ritual_gesture / still.
- **Anti-patterns:** Four-on-floor invent; pop candy cast; strobe on adagio; weapons unless war-score gated; fake chorus on continuous form.

## 8) Electronic / EDM
- **Core pleasure:** Tension→drop catharsis; four-on-floor hypnosis; laser/club body; one earned bloom.
- **Body:** Bounce → freeze on rise → explode on drop; arms, jump, laser-follow eyes.
- **Lyric role:** Often sparse / vocal chops; hook phrase as stamp; instrumental passages OWN the frame on build/drop.
- **World / cast / dance:** Futuristic city, LED wall, space, storm on drop; `neon_runner`, `pop_candy`, formation; HIGH dance.
- **Anti-patterns:** Soft country porch; spoken intimate still through the drop; weapons; constant white-out spam (psych: spend climax once); pastoral animals on big-room peak.

## 8b) Ambient / IDM (electronic siblings)
- **Core pleasure (ambient):** Immersion — dissolve into pads; time stretches; no demand to “peak.”
- **Core pleasure (IDM):** Curious tension; glitch as thought; pattern that refuses easy catharsis.
- **Body:** Ambient float / still; IDM micro-twitch, not club arms.
- **Lyric role:** Minimal or absent; silence and texture are the lyric.
- **World / cast / dance:** Dream clouds, white/soft void, space; `void_presence`, fauna; still / anti-dance.
- **Anti-patterns:** Forced EDM drop; formation cheer; metal war; karaoke urgency.

## 9) Latin / reggaeton / samba
- **Core pleasure:** Dance-color — hips, clave/ dembow pocket, carnival warmth, body as the show.
- **Body:** Hip-led groove; partner/ freestyle fire; formation on hooks welcome.
- **Lyric role:** Party, desire, pride, place; hook chants stamp; verses can flirt.
- **World / cast / dance:** Warm city, ocean, sunset desert, carnival light; `fg_performer`, `pop_candy`; dance HIGH.
- **Anti-patterns:** Cold dread void; spoken stillness through dembow; metal ash; English-pop candy that erases clave feel.

## 10) Afrobeat / dancehall / reggae
- **Core pleasure:** Pocket & resistance-joy; bass conversation; toasting spark; body in the one-drop or afro pulse.
- **Body:** Sway, skank, shoulder; groove first, spectacle second.
- **Lyric role:** Toast / chant / roots story; speechLike pockets OK without killing dance.
- **World / cast / dance:** Ocean, warm rain city, forest dusk, dream clouds; freestyle HIGH; formation on hooks.
- **Anti-patterns:** Fake EDM white-out spam; industrial metal default; weapons drip; sterile white void with no warmth.

## 11) K-pop / J-pop
- **Core pleasure:** Formation spectacle; costume-color clarity; hook as synchronized smile-machine; MV set-piece grammar.
- **Body:** Precision dance — every limb readable; chorus = formation peak.
- **Lyric role:** Hook slogan huge; bilingual sparkle; verses set character/costume world.
- **World / cast / dance:** Futuristic city, candy void, LED, soft space; `formation_crew` MAX; dance VERY HIGH.
- **Anti-patterns:** Sparse jazz negative-space as default; metal chaos; spoken clamp; weapons; muddy illegible group silhouettes.

## 12) Gospel / worship
- **Core pleasure:** Lift — communal hope; light as grace; swell from hush to glory without violence.
- **Body:** Hands rise, sway, choir geometry; tears OK; not mosh.
- **Lyric role:** Testimony / praise owns meaning; type can be title-integrated on lifts; never ironic dread.
- **World / cast / dance:** Cathedral, dream clouds, soft space, ocean light; `sacred_solitary` → choir `formation_crew`; ritual + joy sway.
- **Anti-patterns:** Weapons; scary packs; chaos fracture; strobe war; cynical noir as default.

## 13) Indie / alt
- **Core pleasure:** Bittersweet honesty; DIY intimacy; landscape of feeling over spectacle.
- **Body:** Sway / walk / still — anti-cheer.
- **Lyric role:** Diary / oblique poetry; scenic type; don’t force stadium banner.
- **World / cast / dance:** Rain city, highway, forest, ruins, snow; `tableau_figure`, `pastoral_walker`; still/walk.
- **Anti-patterns:** K-pop formation default; EDM drop spam; candy oversaturation; war drip.

## 14) Soundtrack / trailer
- **Core pleasure:** Designed arc — tease → hit → aftermath; leitmotif return; “movie already playing.”
- **Body:** Still watcher’s spine; hit = flinch then settle; dance only if diegetic.
- **Lyric role:** Often none; title cards; when vocal, treat as diegetic character.
- **World / cast / dance:** Match fantasy narrative; cinematic cast; dance rare.
- **Anti-patterns:** Pop earworm sparkle fighting a dread cue; unearned second hit; club formation on funeral cue.

## 15) Spoken-word / non-groove
- **Core pleasure:** Lyric-as-world — every image lands; intimacy of being spoken to; silence that listens.
- **Body:** Still; micro-gesture; breath.
- **Lyric role:** DOMINANT — never fake chorus; type and figure serve words.
- **World / cast / dance:** Soft void, intimate room, forest/ocean hush; `spoken_intimate`; stillness.
- **Anti-patterns:** Drop catharsis; formation dance; 808 invent; weapons; strobe.

## 16) Experimental / noise
- **Core pleasure:** Unease / texture as truth; refusal of comfort; earned chaos (not decoration).
- **Body:** Flitch, freeze, anti-dance; harsh in the skin.
- **Lyric role:** Fragment / absent / screamed; don’t slap pop hooks.
- **World / cast / dance:** Red/white void, fracture, tunnel, staircase; `void_presence`, `chaos_fracture`; glitch body.
- **Anti-patterns:** Gospel lift; candy formation; pastoral animals; fake four-on-floor “to help”; gun glyphs as cheap spice.

---

## Pleasure → show law (all genres)
1. **Give the chase** the family came for (hook / pocket / swell / story / drop / intimacy / formation / wordplay).
2. **Don’t steal** another family’s signature pleasure as the default.
3. **Lyrics + live vibe** can bend a family (a country song can get scary); anti-patterns are defaults to avoid, not absolute bans on lyric-led exceptions.
4. **Psych:** anticipation earns peak; soothe earns next peak — even in EDM and metal.
