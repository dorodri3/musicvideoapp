## Scene Director

- Treat dance as `cast.action` family: `freestyle` | `formation` | `ritual_gesture` | `still` | `walk`.
- **Dance wins:** groove / euphoric chorus / club / joy-ritual → `fg_performer` center-framed dancer, or `formation_crew` on hook, or `neon_runner`/`pop_candy` freestyle.
- **Stillness wins:** spoken, dread/scary, pastoral calm, analytic sad verse → `still` / slow `walk`; micro-gesture max.
- Pre/build: contained pulse only — full formation waits for chorus (anticipation).
- Drop: optional 1-beat hit pose; don’t open a dance break unless lyrics/vibe demand.
- Pair with ROLE-AGENTS: kick→footfalls of the dancer; snare→clap/accent companion in formation; hats→sparks around the body.
