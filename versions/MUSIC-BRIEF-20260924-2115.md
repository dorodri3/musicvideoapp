## Scene Director

**Section arcs (from Murai long-take chapters + Matsoukas motif islands + Pozzi cut density)**  
- Drive intents from structure: intro → verse → pre → chorus → drop → breakdown → outro.  
- Verse: develop **one** world; plant 2–4 motifs (object, color temp, weather, silhouette).  
- Chorus: **same world opened** — motifStrength↑ on `chorus.repeat` (Formation return / Gondry palindrome), not a random new preset.  
- Drop: single **gate event** (This Is America reset / Glazer fire flip) — one hard transition, then commit.  
- Breakdown: Fury Road “poetic rest” — hold pads fog, restore readability before final chorus.

**Hard-cut vs morph**  
- Default **morph/continuity** inside a section (Seiple Steadicam grammar).  
- Hard-cut allowed: pre→chorus, drop gate, thunder-class `harsh` peaks, tableau-to-tableau Formation-style chapter swaps.  
- Forbidden: hard-cut every kick in a soft verse.

**Prompts**  
1. “Hold warehouse/highway continuity; escalate background weather while lead subject stays center.”  
2. “On chorus.repeat, enlarge planted motifs; do not teleport worlds.”  
3. “At drop, one structural reset then plateau — no new planet every bar.”

## Audio Pulse

**Thresholds (build / drop / silence)** — inspired by arXiv sync habits + Light Show envelopes  
- **Verse:** raise kick/onset thresholds — avoid false shake on pocket drums (storytelling endurance).  
- **Pre:** let `build` ramp; snare fills may **anticipate** chorus downbeat by a few frames (Pozzi off-beat climax tool — use sparingly).  
- **Chorus:** balanced thresholds; keep micro-variation if energy plateaus at 1.0 (held anthem ≠ frozen).  
- **Drop:** fire `drop` as a **short pulse**, not sticky high; pair with kick+harsh.  
- **Silence / near-silence:** suppress sparks; hold pad fog or black pools (2049 negative space).  
- Blend `VocalEstimate` into lead emphasis; never replace `roles.lead`.

**Prompts**  
1. “Expose clean roles + build/drop/silence/beat every frame.”  
2. “Gate drop as impulse; verse kick hysteresis high.”  
3. “On silence=true, zero hats sparks and harsh unless lyric demands ash.”
