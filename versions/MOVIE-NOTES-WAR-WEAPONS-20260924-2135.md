## What to steal

### 1) Childish Gambino — “This Is America” (2018), dir. Hiro Murai / DP Larkin Seiple
- **Sources:** Mulcahey, *Filmmaker Magazine* (2018); ASC / Seiple interview; Dazed / cultural readings of gun-vs-body care.  
  https://filmmakermagazine.com/105396-dp-larkin-seiple-breaks-down-every-shot-from-childish-gambinos-this-is-america/  
  https://theasc.com/article/dp-larkin-seiple-on-this-is-america-quot/
- **Steal:** Weapon enters as a **ruthless tonal cut** inside a continuous take — joy → violence in a blink; gun handled with care while bodies are discarded (meaning = values, not FPS fantasy). FG performer owns frame; BG war chaos does not steal crosshair.
- **Light Show:** `weaponId` only at lyric/concept gates (gun, riot, war words) or chaos vibe peaks; flash then continue — don’t linger fetishizing the prop. Cast: `fg_performer` / `chaos_fracture`.

### 2) *The Hurt Locker* (2008), dir. Kathryn Bigelow / DP Barry Ackroyd
- **Sources:** Time in Pixels cinematography notes; Frames Cinema Journal on Hurt Locker / Apocalypse Now trauma spectacle; JWCS “Embodiment in the war film.”  
  https://timeinpixels.com/2015/08/cinematography-in-the-hurt-locker/  
  https://framescinemajournal.com/article/things-that-almost-killed-me-apocalypse-now-and-the-hurt-locker-and-the-influence-of-19th-century-spectacle-art-in-the-war-film/
- **Steal:** Bomb suit / triggers as **embodiment** — weapon-tech isolates the body; POV through scope/suit; observers on rooftops = dread; ECUs of eyes/shells for tension. War as addictive cycle, not parade.
- **Light Show:** `ash_survivor` + heavy silhouette / suit weight; slow dread push; rare startle. Weapons = burden props (`ied_burden`, `scope_pov` flash) not spray-fire. Scary/chaos aftermath vibes.

### 3) *Apocalypse Now* (1979), dir. Francis Ford Coppola
- **Sources:** Frames Cinema Journal (phantasmagoria / battlefield haunting vs panorama).
- **Steal:** War as **surreal haunting** — episodic return to trauma; spectacle that rewrites psyche; weapons/aircraft as mythic set pieces inside a fever dream, not tactical checklist.
- **Light Show:** Warzone packs can go phantasmagoric (smoke shafts, silhouetted choppers as environmental agents) when lyric is apocalyptic/journey; pads fog + harsh ash; avoid documentary HUD.

### 4) *Mad Max: Fury Road* (2015), dir. George Miller
- **Sources:** VashiVisuals / Polygon on center-frame (“crosshairs on the gun”); Motion Picture Assoc. / Autoweek on War Rig — weapons positioned as vehicle/story function.  
  https://vashivisuals.com/the-editing-of-mad-max-fury-road/  
  https://www.polygon.com/2015/6/1/8700423/watch-how-mad-max-used-center-framing-to-keep-the-action-scenes/
- **Steal:** **Crosshair the weapon or the face** so chaos stays readable; props grown from world function (scrap logic), not floating gun stickers.
- **Light Show:** If `weaponId` active, keep it on center with the cast; chaos_fracture + warzone vehicles as scenic agents; kick = ground punch under feet still wins over muzzle flash spam.

### 5) *Children of Men* (2006), dir. Alfonso Cuarón
- **Sources:** *Senses of Cinema* — focalisation / long-take warzone refugee grammar.  
  https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2014/feature-articles/focalization-realism-and-narrative-asymmetry-in-alfonso-cuarons-children-of-men/
- **Steal:** Camera walks *through* conflict; weapons and checkpoints as **world texture** around the human crosshair; attention distributed — not FPS.
- **Light Show:** Morph continuity through warzone; lyric figure walks; rifles as BG environmental props until a concept forces a foreground beat.
